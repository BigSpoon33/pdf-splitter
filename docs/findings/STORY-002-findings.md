## Gate r1 fixes (`4d8d375`)

Review round 1 (`docs/findings/STORY-002-review.md`) confirmed three findings; all three are fixed forward on `feature/web-mode`, no other behaviour changed, and the review's repro scripts (probe3/4/5, real5) now give the expected output.

1. **Straddling wraps merge** (finding 1, AC-3). `heading_candidates` bucketed wrap lines by the 3-way `col` class, so a wrapped heading with one line wider than `full_width_ratio·W` and one narrower never joined. Lines now bucket by the side of `column_split` their `x0` falls on (`detect.py: _side`), which is `locate_heading`'s grouping (`index.py:229`), and the merged candidate's `col` is `full` if any of its lines is full, else that side. Size tolerance, `wrap_gap` and the 3-line cap are unchanged. Tests: `test_a_wrapped_heading_straddling_the_full_width_threshold_is_one_full_candidate` (wide→narrow and narrow→wide, plus a same-size line on the other side inside `wrap_gap` that must NOT join), and a genuinely one-column synthetic book, `tests/fixtures.py: single_column_book` (US Letter, 72 pt margin, 20 pt chapter titles wrapped at 24 pt leading, 13 pt sections, 14 pt running header, roman folios `i`/`ii`/`iii`): `test_single_column_book_chapters_are_one_candidate_each_under_both_geometries` (default and `single_column` geometry; every chapter `full`) and `test_single_column_candidates_open_a_book_and_every_heading_is_located` (round-trip with `profile_from_dict({"single_column": True, "heading_wrap_gap": 24})`, 0 `heading-not-found`). 2. **Roman page numbers only at the page edge** (finding 2, AC-3/AC-6). `_PAGE_NUMBER`'s `[ivxlcdm]+` swallowed any word made of those letters. Now `_DIGIT_PAGE` (`12`, `- 12 -`, `Page 3`, `— 7 —`) excludes anywhere on the page; `_ROMAN_PAGE` excludes only a **well-formed** numeral (`m{0,3}(cm|cd|d?c{0,3})(xc|xl|l?x{0,3})(ix|iv|v?i{0,3})`, optional `page`) that sits in the page-edge strip `_running` uses (`_at_edge`: `max(band, 0.12·H)` at either end). The old test placed `xiv` mid-page at 20 pt and expected exclusion; under the new rule that is a heading, so the roman cases moved to the page edge.

## Gate r2 fixes (`defb734`)

Review round 2 (`docs/findings/STORY-002-review.md` § Round 2) confirmed two findings with one root cause: `_at_edge` floored the page-edge strip at `0.12·H` (≈ 95 pt on Letter) regardless of the caller's bands, so page-opening headings (top ≈ 60–70 pt) sat "at the edge" — roman-letter glossary headings (C D I L M V X) were dropped as folios, and digit-masked "Lesson 1..10" on ≥ 30% of pages collapsed into one "running header". Shuma approved one targeted fix (attempt 3, binding rules 1–5 in the kickoff); it is one commit forward on `feature/web-mode`, and nothing outside `detect.py`, its tests, the single-column fixture and the README paragraph changed.

**Interpretation of AC-6 recorded here (binding rules 1–3):**
- **The caller's bands are the only page edge.** `EDGE_SHARE`, `_at_edge` and the floor are deleted. A line with top inside `[0, header_band)` or `[H − footer_band, H)` is out; a line outside is never second-guessed by its position.
- **Folios.** Digit-only lines (`12`, `- 12 -`, `Page 12`) are page numbers anywhere (unchanged). A well-formed roman numeral is a page number **only at the edge** — and under rule 1 the edge is the band, whose filter already removes every line in it. The roman branch could therefore never fire after the band filter, so `_ROMAN_PAGE` and `_page_number` were removed rather than left as unreachable code; the *behaviour* is exactly rule 2 and is pinned by `test_roman_folios_inside_the_default_bands_are_never_candidates` (`xiv`/`XIV`/`- iv -`/`Page iv` at baseline 782, top ≈ 760.6 ≥ H − 32; `mcmxcix` at baseline 40, top ≈ 18.6 < 50) and `test_a_roman_numeral_is_a_folio_only_inside_the_callers_bands` (`xiv` at the foot of the page with bands 0 is a heading).
- **Running headers, two kinds.** (a) *Verbatim*: identical normalised text (case/whitespace-folded, digits NOT masked) at the same size on ≥ max(2, ⌈0.3·pages⌉) pages → excluded anywhere, counted over every line of every page (`_running`, one key per page). (b) *Digit-varying* ("Chapter 3 · Title  45"): excluded only at the edge — which is, again, the band filter. **Consequence, accepted:** with `header_band = footer_band = 0` a digit-varying running header is no longer auto-excluded; the user's bands define the edge, and the section list is user-editable (PRD A-1). No separate code path exists for (b) for the same reason as the roman rule.
- The size stays in the running key, so a 16 pt chapter title at the top of its first page is never confused with the 9 pt running header that repeats its words.

**Tests (rule 4), all in `tests/test_detect.py`:** `test_an_a_to_z_glossary_of_page_opening_letters_keeps_every_letter` — letters at baseline 90 (top ≈ 68.6, the r2 geometry) → 26 candidates, one level; `test_a_workbook_of_numbered_lessons_opening_every_third_page_keeps_every_lesson` — 30 pages, "Lesson 1..10" 20 pt at baseline 90 with a 9 pt "Practical Workbook" running line, default bands → exactly the 10 lessons; `test_a_line_repeated_verbatim_on_30_percent_of_pages_is_running_even_with_no_bands` ("Rare Header" on 2/10 pages → two headings; on 3/10 → none, bands 0); `test_numbered_lines_are_not_a_running_header_only_the_callers_band_drops_them` ("Rare Header 1/2/3" on 3/10 pages: kept with bands 0, dropped by the default header band); `test_digit_page_numbers_are_never_candidates_anywhere_even_with_no_bands`; `test_roman_folios_inside_the_default_bands_are_never_candidates`; `test_running_headers_and_page_numbers_are_never_candidates_even_with_no_bands` (the headed book's verbatim 14 pt header, unchanged). `single_column_book`'s roman folios moved from baseline 760 (top ≈ 746.6, outside the default footer band — they were only "at the edge" thanks to the deleted strip) to 778 (top ≈ 764.6, inside it), and `test_single_column_book_chapters_are_one_candidate_each_under_both_geometries` now asserts both halves of the rule: with bands 0 the verbatim running header is still out and `i`/`ii`/`iii` are in; with the default bands the folios are out. The orchestrator's repros (`probe_glossary2.py`: 26/26 at baseline 90 and 120 and with bands 0; `probe_numbered2.py`: 10/10 under every setting) give the expected output. Suite: **129 passed** (126 → 129: 12 tests replaced by 15).

## Bugs Found
- None in the new code.
- **Pre-existing, matters for web mode (STORY-003):** `Book.excerpt_path(name)` = `out / f"{name}.pdf"` uses the **raw entry name as a filename**. A detected name containing `/` (Maciocia's candidates include `https://evolve.elsevier.com/Maciocia/foundations/`) writes into a subdirectory or fails, and `../` in a user-edited name escapes `out`. Duplicate names ("Notes", "Introduction" and "End Notes" repeat across Maciocia's L2) overwrite one another's PDF. `Book.entry(name)` and `overrides.json` are also keyed by name, so the second duplicate can't be addressed. Detection returns names as they are and does not dedupe. The web layer or `cut_all` must make names unique and filename-safe.
